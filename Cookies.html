Cookie Policy

Aurinko Re informs users about the use of cookies on its website.

Cookies are files that can be downloaded to your device through web pages. They are essential tools for providing many services of the information society. Among other functions, they allow a website to store and retrieve information about a user’s browsing habits or their device and, depending on the information obtained, they may be used to recognize the user and improve the service provided.

Types of Cookies

Depending on the entity that manages the domain from which cookies are sent and processes the data obtained, two types can be distinguished: first-party cookies and third-party cookies.

There is also another classification based on the length of time cookies remain stored in the user’s browser: session cookies and persistent cookies.

Finally, cookies can also be classified into five categories according to their purpose: technical cookies, personalization cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, and behavioral advertising cookies.

Cookies Used on the Website

The cookies used on this website, along with their type and function, are described below:

The Aurinko Re website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, which enables the measurement and analysis of website usage. In your browser, you may observe cookies associated with this service.

Through web analytics, information is obtained regarding the number of users accessing the website, the number of page views, the frequency and recurrence of visits, their duration, the browser used, the service provider, the language, the device used, and the city associated with the IP address. This information enables Aurinko Re to provide a better and more appropriate service.

Another cookie used is a technical cookie called PHPSESSID, which stores a unique identifier per session to enable navigation.

Additionally, two technical cookies named cookieControl and cookieControlPrefs are used to manage user consent regarding cookies and remember user preferences.
